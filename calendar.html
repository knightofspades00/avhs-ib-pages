Calendar & Timeline

Four years, mapped out.

The IB roadmap from 9th grade through the May exams of senior year. Plus key deadlines you'll want on your fridge — and the rhythms of an IB family's year.

Specific dates change each year.

This is a structural calendar — when things typically happen during an IB student's four-year journey. For exact dates this year (exam windows, EE deadlines, parent nights), check with the IB Coordinator. The IB publishes the official May exam schedule each January.

9th grade — MYP Year 1

The foundation year. Students adjust to high school, learn the MYP framework, and start building habits.

August / September

Welcome to IB

Orientation. Introduction to the IB Learner Profile. First teacher meetings with the GLA / IB cohort.

Fall semester

MYP framework in action

Subject teachers introduce ATL skills, Global Contexts, and criterion-based assessment. First Service as Action reflections.

Winter

First IB Parent Information Night

The big "what is IB" night for 9th-grade families. Spanish interpretation provided.

Spring

End-of-year reflection

Students reflect on their first year — what worked, what didn't, how they're growing in the Learner Profile.

10th grade — MYP Year 2 (Capstone Year)

The MYP capstone year. Students complete the Personal Project and prepare to step into the Diploma Programme.

Fall semester

Personal Project launches

Students choose a project goal, find a supervisor, and start the multi-month process. First proposals submitted.

Winter

Course selection for the DP

Students start identifying their six subjects for 11th grade. HL choices considered carefully. One-on-one meetings with the Coordinator.

Spring

Personal Project completion + Exhibition Night

Final projects presented to the school community. Family and friends invited. This is the public face of MYP.

May / June

MYP Certificate awarded

Students who completed the MYP requirements receive their MYP Certificate. Transition into Diploma Programme orientation.

11th grade — DP Year 1

The Diploma Programme begins. Six subjects, TOK launches, EE gets seeded, CAS opens.

August

DP starts — TOK begins

First TOK class. CAS portfolio opened. Hawks get their official IB course registration numbers.

Fall semester

Settling into HL/SL

Students start noticing the difference between HL and SL — depth, pacing, expectations. Adjustments to course load can be made in early fall.

Winter

Internal Assessments (IAs) begin

Several subjects start their IA work in the second semester of junior year. These are the major projects that count toward your IB grade.

Spring

Extended Essay topic selection

Students choose their EE subject area, draft an initial research question, and identify a supervisor. Summer reading lists assigned.

May / June

End of junior year

Most IAs in progress, EE in early stages. Students leave for the summer with a research plan and reading goals.

The summer between 11 and 12

This summer matters. Hawks who use it well make senior year significantly easier.

  • EE research and reading. Sources collected. Notes started. Outline drafted.
  • IA work continued. Some IAs benefit from quiet summer drafting.
  • College list and applications. Start thinking about where you'll apply. Visit campuses if possible. Common App opens in August.
  • Rest. Yes — actual rest. You're going to need it.

12th grade — DP Year 2 (The Big Year)

The diploma year. EE final draft, IAs completed, TOK essay submitted, college applications, and the May exams.

August / September

Back to school + college applications

Senior year begins. UC applications open in October, Common App is already open. EE work continues. CAS still ongoing.

October

EE first draft due (typical)

The first complete draft of your Extended Essay is due to your supervisor. Feedback comes back. Major revisions begin.

November

UC applications due (November 30)

UC and CSU applications submitted. Common App early-decision/early-action deadlines fall in November as well.

December / January

EE and IAs nearing completion

Final EE submissions and most major IAs locked. TOK essay draft started. Winter break is intentional rest time.

February / March

TOK essay submitted · CAS portfolio finalized

TOK essay (1,600 words) uploaded to the IB. CAS Coordinator signs off on the CAS portfolio. Most IAs submitted.

April

Exam preparation intensive

Subject teachers run review sessions. Past papers practice. Sleep matters more than ever.

May

IB EXAMS

2–3 weeks of external IB exams. The world's IB students all take them together. After exams: real rest.

June

Graduation

AVHS graduation. The IB Diploma itself is awarded over the summer once scores come in.

Early July

IB scores released

Students log in to receive their official IB results. The IB sends scores directly to the universities you designated.

Annual rhythms — the year in IB

Every IB family develops a feel for the year. These are the predictable beats:

August: launch

Fresh start. New classes, new teachers. The first 2 weeks set the habits for the year. Build them deliberately.

October: first reality check

The honeymoon ends. Major assignments hit. Some students stumble. This is normal — and recoverable.

November–December: deadline cluster

UC applications. EE draft. First IAs. The hardest stretch of fall. Plan for it.

January–February: deep IA season

The longest internal stretch. Quiet, sustained work. The most "IB" part of the IB year.

March–April: exam ramp

Review sessions, practice papers, mock exams. Sleep, food, hydration become study strategies.

May: exams + release

The exams themselves. Then — for seniors — a sudden release. Some of the most memorable Hawk weeks ever.

Where to find official dates

  • IB May Exam Schedule: Published by the IB each January. Check with the Coordinator or the IB's official site.
  • UC/CSU application deadlines: UC and CSU applications typically open August 1 and close November 30.
  • Common App: Opens August 1 each year. School-specific deadlines vary widely.
  • AVHS school calendar: See arroyovalley.sbcusd.com for school holidays and minimum days.
  • SBCUSD magnet lottery: See the application page for current lottery dates.
