We are Arroyo Valley Hawks.

Twenty-five years on Baseline Street, in the shadow of the San Bernardino Mountains. Generations of bilingual, hard-working, first-generation Hawks. A school that bet on a world-class diploma — and a community flying higher because of it.

  • We are builders. First-generation college-bound. Bilingual at the kitchen table. Doing the work, every day, because our families showed us how.
  • We are scholars. Taking on the most rigorous diploma in the world — not because anyone gave it to us, but because we earned the right to ask for it.
  • We are community. When one Hawk rises, we all rise. The valley raised us; we represent the valley wherever we go.
  • We are global. Our flock graduates to UCs, CSUs, private universities, the trades, the military, and into the world — carrying San Bernardino with us.

Five quick answers

If you only have five minutes, these are the things every family wants to know first.

$

How much does it cost?

IB at Arroyo Valley is part of your free public education. There are no tuition fees. Exam fees, when they apply, are typically covered by the district.

⏱

How long is the program?

Two tracks: MYP in 9th–10th grade, then the Diploma Programme (DP) in 11th–12th. Students can join the IB pathway in 9th grade.

🎓

Does it help with college?

Yes. Universities recognize IB worldwide. The University of California and Cal State systems award college credit for qualifying scores. See credit policy →

✦

How do I apply?

Through the SBCUSD magnet lottery. AVHS is one of two IB lottery schools in the district. See application steps →

The two programmes

One pathway. Two stages. Built for growth.

The IB at Arroyo Valley is a four-year journey. The first two years (the Middle Years Programme) build skills, mindset, and inquiry. The last two years (the Diploma Programme) ask students to put it all together — and graduate ready for anything that comes next.

Grades 9–10

Middle Years Programme (MYP)

The MYP is where Hawks learn how to think, ask, and connect. Eight subject groups, real-world global contexts, and a capstone Personal Project in 10th grade.

  • Interdisciplinary units across all classes
  • Approaches-to-Learning (ATL) skills
  • Service as Action
  • Personal Project — your idea, your voice
Explore the MYP →
Grades 11–12

Diploma Programme (DP)

The Diploma Programme is two years of focused, college-level study across six subject groups, plus the DP core: Theory of Knowledge, the Extended Essay, and CAS.

  • 6 subjects (3 Higher Level, 3 Standard Level)
  • Theory of Knowledge (TOK)
  • Extended Essay — your own 4,000-word research paper
  • CAS — Creativity, Activity, Service

Why Arroyo Valley

An IB program that's for San Bernardino.

Arroyo Valley is one of only two IB high schools in the San Bernardino City Unified School District. We believe a world-class education shouldn't be reserved for a zip code or a price tag — it should be the standard for the kids growing up in our neighborhood, speaking the languages of our families, and shaping the future of our city.

Our IB students go on to UC and CSU campuses, private universities, community college, the trades, the military, and entrepreneurship. The diploma doesn't just open doors — it teaches Hawks how to walk through them with confidence and conviction.

Who we're building

The IB Learner Profile — 10 attributes our Hawks practice every day.

Every IB student in the world — from Helsinki to Hong Kong to San Bernardino — works toward the same ten attributes. These aren't slogans; they shape how Hawks read, write, argue, serve, and live.

?

Inquirers

Curious. Independent learners. Love figuring things out.

📚

Knowledgeable

Deep across disciplines. Local and global awareness.

🧠

Thinkers

Critical and creative. Reasoned, ethical decisions.

💬

Communicators

In more than one language. Listen as well as we speak.

⚖

Principled

Honesty, fairness, dignity — for ourselves and everyone.

🌎

Open-minded

Value our own culture. Seek out other points of view.

❤

Caring

Show empathy. Act to make a positive difference.

↯

Risk-takers

Approach uncertainty with courage and forethought.

⚖

Balanced

Intellect, body, heart — and the people around us.

↻

Reflective

Honest about strengths and limits. Always learning.
