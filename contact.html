How to apply to the IB program at AVHS

AVHS is one of two SBCUSD magnet schools that offer the IB program. Students apply through the district's magnet lottery system. Here's the process:

Step 1

Visit a parent information night (recommended, not required)

Before applying, come to an IB info night to hear directly from the Coordinator, meet teachers, and ask questions. Spanish interpretation is provided. Dates are posted on the AVHS website and on the SBCUSD magnet page in the fall.

Step 2

Submit a magnet lottery application

Applications are submitted through SBCUSD's online magnet application portal. The application window typically opens in the fall and closes in early spring. You can apply to AVHS regardless of where you live within SBCUSD boundaries.

SBCUSD magnet application ↗

Step 3

Lottery selection

If applications exceed available seats, students are selected via lottery. Selection criteria, sibling preference rules, and notification timelines are managed by SBCUSD. You'll be notified by the district.

Step 4

Acceptance and enrollment

Accepted students enroll at AVHS and join the Global Leadership Academy / IB pathway. The school registrar handles enrollment paperwork; the IB Coordinator handles program-specific orientation.

Step 5

Orientation

New IB students and families receive orientation materials, course-selection guidance, and a chance to meet teachers and the IB cohort before the school year begins.

Specific dates vary each year.

The exact magnet application window and lottery dates are set by SBCUSD annually. For the current year's dates, check the SBCUSD magnet page or call the IB office.

Transfer students

Already at another high school and want to switch to AVHS for the IB program?

  • Transferring into 9th or 10th grade (MYP) is the smoothest path. Apply through the magnet lottery.
  • Transferring into 11th grade (DP) is possible but requires careful course mapping. Contact the Coordinator before applying so we can review your transcript and confirm the subject sequence will work.
  • Transferring into 12th grade typically can't lead to the full IB Diploma — but students can sometimes take individual IB courses as Course Candidates.

Want to visit campus?

Tours and visits can be arranged through the IB office. Bring your student. Walk the halls. Sit in on a class. The best way to know if AVHS is right for your family is to see it.
